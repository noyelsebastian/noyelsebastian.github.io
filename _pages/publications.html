Working Papers

Homes of Harm: Spousal Violence and Child Malnutrition in India (with Rashmi Barua)
This study investigates the causal impact of intimate partner violence (IPV) experienced by mothers on the nutritional status of children under age five in India. To address endogeneity, IPV is instrumented using the district-level presence of an All-Women Police Station.
