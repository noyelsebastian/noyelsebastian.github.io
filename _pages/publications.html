Homes of Harm: Spousal Violence and Child Malnutrition in India

With Rashmi Barua — Under Review

{% comment %}
This study investigates the causal impact of intimate partner violence (IPV) experienced by mothers on the nutritional status of children under age five in India. IPV increases the probability of stunting by 34.5 percentage points and underweight by 26.9 percentage points.
