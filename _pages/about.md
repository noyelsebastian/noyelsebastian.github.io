I’m a doctoral candidate in Economics at Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi, expected to complete in December 2026. I am on the 2026–27 academic job market. My [CV](/files/NOYEL_CV.pdf) and [job market paper](/files/Sebastian_JMP.pdf) are available here. My research focuses on the intersection of labor markets, health, and family dynamics, with a specific interest in how history, culture, social norms, and institutions shape economic development. I employ applied microeconomic methods to study how violence, social norms, and institutional structures affect health outcomes and women’s labor supply. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Loyola College, Chennai, and a Master’s degree from the University of Kerala, Thiruvananthapuram.

Beyond academia, I enjoy reading non-fiction, experimenting with new recipes, and participating in quizzes. I’m also passionate about travel and fitness. I maintain an [alternate space](https://noyelsebastian.substack.com/) where I write about topics beyond research.
