Microeconomics Tutorial Series

These slides are a part of the Teaching Assistant work for Microeconomics 1 course for CSWE(CITD) graduate students. They serve as a conceptual refresher for the course.[cite: 4, 276, 561]. While other tutorial sessions were conducted without slides, these materials provide a structured review of the core mathematical and economic frameworks discussed in class.

I: Basic Topology

A review of Euclidean spaces, open/closed sets, and limit points. Essential foundations for formal economic proofs.

Download Part I

II: Preference Relations

Formalizing preference orderings, indifference classes, and the unique properties of lexicographic preferences.

Download Part II

III: Convexity & Demand

Connecting convex preferences to the Marshallian Demand set. Includes proofs on singleton demand.

Download Part III
