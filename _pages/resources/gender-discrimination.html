Gender Identity and Discrimination

This was a two-part lecture series conducted by me as part of the Labor Economics elective for CITD graduate students during the Winter 2023 semester. The lectures provide an overview of gender differences in the labor market, focusing on theoretical and empirical frameworks for understanding the disparities.
